Framing guides

Looking for your camera…
  1. Switch the camera on. If the battery is low: it only charges over USB while switched off, so charge it first (or use a dummy battery for long sessions).
  2. PC Remote on. MENU › Network › PC Remote Function › PC Remote: On. Wi-Fi: connection method “Access Point” (camera on your home Wi-Fi). Or just plug in the USB cable.
  3. That's it. Studio keeps trying and connects by itself. Nothing else to do.

Photo preview
Makes the photo preview and the JPEG reference flat like your S-Log3 video. The RAW file is not changed. Cost: minimum ISO 500 in photo mode (instead of 100), so in bright light the RAW gets less of its cleanest range.
See the exposure
Read from the picture as the camera sends it (S-Log3 levels in video): grey card green 39–43, skin pink 48–56, yellow 80–90, red near clip.
